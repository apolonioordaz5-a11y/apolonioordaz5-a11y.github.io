Electric propulsion / Manufacturing

PEPPR Hall-Effect Thruster

HET-01 development | Manufacturing and test readiness

Electric PropulsionCADManufacturingMagnetic TestingVacuum Testing

I am working with PEPPR to manufacture and integrate a sub-kilowatt Hall-effect thruster. The full design has not yet fired, but the team has prepared the vacuum chamber and test setup and characterized the magnet system ahead of full-thruster testing.

01

Development status

Build hardware, verify subsystems, then test the integrated thruster

ManufacturingMechanical hardware is in production and integration is underway.
Test readinessThe vacuum chamber and test setup have been prepared.
MagneticsThe magnetic system has been characterized before full operation.
Next milestone: complete integration and conduct the first full-thruster test.
02

First-pass design point

Scaling-law model used to size the initial concept

660 WModel discharge power
300 VModel discharge voltage
39 mNModel thrust

The initial geometry uses a sub-kilowatt Hall-thruster scaling approach: a 59 mm mean channel diameter, 14 mm channel width, and 25 mm channel length. These are model-based design inputs, not measured performance results.

03

What the project is testing

From a credible design model to an operating propulsion system

The work connects propulsion sizing, magnetic-field geometry, component manufacturing, and vacuum-facility preparation. Full-thruster operation will validate how the manufactured system performs as an integrated assembly.
