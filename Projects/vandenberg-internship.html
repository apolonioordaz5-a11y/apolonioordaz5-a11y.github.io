Internship / Rapid prototyping

Vandenberg Space Force Base

Rapid Prototyping & Manufacturing Engineering Intern | Summer 2026

3D Scanning CNC Machining Composites Fabrication Restoration

At Vandenberg Space Force Base, I interned with the Training Device Design and Engineering Center (TDDEC). My individual project was restoring the base’s P-40 display aircraft: rebuilding its propellers and nose-cone support after the original struts rusted away.

01

Who is TDDEC?

Rapid repair and construction of training equipment

TDDEC is a unique flight within the U.S. Space Force that contributes heavily to the rapid repair and construction of mission-critical training equipment. The shop combines woodworking, waterjet cutting, machining, and fabrication under one roof.

02

The problem

P-40 display restoration · individual project

The struts inside the base’s P-40 display had rusted away, causing the propellers to fall from the nose cone. The goal was to rebuild the propellers and their support so the display could be restored to its original appearance and last outdoors.

03

Solution process

Scan, machine, reinforce, assemble

DigitizedCaptured a reference propeller with a 3D scanner
MachinedCut the new propeller blanks on a CNC gantry router
ReinforcedEmbedded stainless-steel rods and added fiberglass
AssembledFit the propellers into the rebuilt nose cone
04

Engineering verification

Checks before the display goes back up

  • Verified nose-cone and propeller alignment during assembly.
  • Evaluated propeller support and internal reinforcement.
  • Validated material selection for long-term outdoor exposure.
Remaining work: prime and paint, reinstall onto the plane, and complete a safety inspection.
05

Impact and legacy

Preserving WWII aviation history on base

RestoredOriginal appearance of the display
ImprovedStrength and durability of the propeller support
ExtendedService life of the display

The restoration preserves a piece of WWII aviation history, reinforces base heritage and pride, and showcases TDDEC’s craftsmanship and preservation work.
